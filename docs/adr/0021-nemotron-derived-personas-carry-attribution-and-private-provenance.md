# Personas drafted from Nemotron records carry attribution and private provenance

Personas may be drafted from Persona seeds taken from the Nemotron-Personas
datasets (Korea, Japan and USA). At the operator's request, source attribution
lives only in a GitHub comment. This replaces the earlier decision to show a
site-wide credit in Discovery's landing footer and Likes panel. Public profiles
remain the persona's own self-presentation.

Each derived persona privately records its dataset, dataset revision and record ID,
so a record is never drafted twice and every derived persona can be traced to its
source. Provenance stays out of Persona documents and versions, public responses and
the Conversation Catalog.
