# Personas drafted from Nemotron records carry attribution and private provenance

Personas may be drafted from Persona seeds taken from NVIDIA's Nemotron-Personas
datasets (Korea, Japan and USA), which are licensed CC BY 4.0. Their public profiles
are adapted material shown to the public, so Discovery carries one credit line that
names NVIDIA, links each dataset and the license, and states that the records were
modified. A credit on every derived profile was rejected: one site-wide notice is a
reasonable manner of attribution for this medium and keeps public profiles as the
persona's own self-presentation.

Each derived persona privately records its dataset, dataset revision and record ID,
so a record is never drafted twice and every derived persona can be traced to its
source. Provenance stays out of Persona documents and versions, public responses and
the Conversation Catalog. The credit line stays for as long as any derived persona
appears in Discovery; removing it first requires removing those personas.
