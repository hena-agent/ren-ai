# Users choose the starting relationship independently of affection

## Decision

Discovery contact requests choose dating, flirting, friends or strangers. Dating
is the default and means newly dating, not a long-established shared history.
The choice belongs to a Conversation rather than the Persona or user's gender.
Friendship is a complete platonic option, not a romance-progression stage.

Persist the choice with its registration and copy it and the registration reference
into the Conversation when first contact begins. Supply it at Greeting, reconstruction
and every model turn. Existing unset Conversations are not retroactively changed.
Later mutually agreed relationship development belongs to Conversation Memory.

## Trade-offs

Requiring all users to progress from strangers through affection-gated events delays
the desired companionship and makes character concepts harder to realize. A chosen
starting premise provides that experience immediately while preserving individual
personality, boundaries and real discovery of user facts.

Relationship and knowledge must therefore be separate: being partners does not imply
knowing occupation or location, and never authorizes fabricated dates or memories.
Affection remains an evolving feeling, not a relationship-unlock threshold. This
refines ADR-0013's initial-affection interpretation without changing its ownership
of State and Memory.
